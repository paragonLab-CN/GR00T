Figure X. Overview of FLARE-VLA. (A) Given an offline demonstration window, GR00T samples K action chunks from the same observation and instruction. LeWM rolls out a latent future for each candidate using the encoded history. The observed future frames are encoded as a ground-truth latent target. Candidates are ranked by mean future-latent MSE, then screened for score agreement, oracle gap, and action quality under a teacher budget. (B) Accepted teacher actions provide additional supervision for the original observations. The full expert dataset is retained and mixed with selected teacher samples to fine-tune GR00T. The illustrated K = 4 and H = 8 match the current LIBERO-Plus setup.
